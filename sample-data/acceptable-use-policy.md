# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** IT Department
**Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of company information technology resources including computers, networks, email, internet access, and cloud services. It exists to protect the organization and its employees from the risks associated with misuse of technology resources.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and any other persons granted access to company IT resources, regardless of location.

## 3. General Use

Company IT resources are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job responsibilities, consume significant resources, or violate any other company policy.

Users are responsible for exercising good judgment regarding the reasonableness of personal use. All data created, stored, or transmitted on company systems is company property and may be monitored.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

- Sharing login credentials or using another person's account
- Installing unauthorized software on company devices
- Connecting unauthorized devices to the corporate network
- Accessing, downloading, or distributing offensive or illegal material
- Using company resources for personal commercial activities
- Circumventing or attempting to circumvent security controls
- Unauthorized access or attempted access to systems or data
- Transmitting classified data through unapproved channels
- Using company email to send unsolicited bulk messages

## 5. Email and Communications

### 5.1 Email Use
Company email must be used for business correspondence. Users should exercise caution with attachments and links from unknown senders. Sensitive information must be encrypted when sent externally.

### 5.2 Messaging
Approved messaging platforms (Slack, Microsoft Teams) may be used for business communications. Confidential information should not be shared through messaging platforms without appropriate channel restrictions.

## 6. Internet Use

Internet access is provided for business purposes. Use of streaming services, social media, and other non-business sites should be limited and not interfere with work. The company may restrict access to certain website categories.

## 7. Mobile Devices

### 7.1 Company-Owned Devices
Company-owned mobile devices must have MDM software installed, device encryption enabled, and screen lock configured with a minimum 6-digit PIN or biometric authentication.

### 7.2 Personal Devices (BYOD)
Personal devices used for company business must meet minimum security requirements including current OS version, device encryption, and screen lock. Company data on personal devices may be remotely wiped upon employment termination.

## 8. Password Requirements

- Minimum 12 characters with complexity requirements
- Passwords must not be reused across systems
- Password managers are recommended and provided by the company
- Passwords must never be shared or written down in accessible locations
- Multi-factor authentication must be enabled where available

## 9. Software Installation

Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices. Software requests are submitted through the IT service desk. All software must be properly licensed. Open-source software requires security review before installation.

## 10. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in temporary or permanent revocation of IT access privileges and disciplinary action up to and including termination. Illegal activities will be reported to appropriate authorities.
